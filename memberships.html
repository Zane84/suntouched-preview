The fine print, in plain words

Billing. Your card is billed on the 1st of every month. Memberships are a three-month minimum. There's no start-up fee and no cancellation fee.

Retail discount. Members get 15% off lotions and every retail product in the salon.

Going away for a while? You can freeze your membership for up to four months a year instead of cancelling. Ask at the desk.

Changing levels. Move up or down a level anytime at the front desk and keep your discount.

Cancelling. Cancellations must be in writing by 11:59pm on the 20th of the month to stop the next month's draft. Use the form on our membership changes page or fill one out in the salon.

Declined cards. If a card declines for three months the membership is cancelled. You can re-join any time.
