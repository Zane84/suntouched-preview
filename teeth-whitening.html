LED teeth whitening

A relaxing 20 to 60 minute session that lifts stains without changing or damaging your teeth. Most people brighten five to eight shades per 20-minute session, up to fourteen in total. About the time of a haircut.

  • Whitening session$175By appointment. We recommend the 60-minute session to remove every layer of staining.

Three steps

  1. Gel

    Whitening gel goes on the upper and lower arch. The DaVinci formula is organic, plant- and mineral-based, and builds enamel as it works.

  2. Light

    The LED light is positioned at your mouth to accelerate the whitening. Sessions run 20 to 60 minutes depending on your staining and how white you want to go.

  3. Rinse

    Rinse and see the result. Every set of teeth has a natural stop point, and results depend on your starting shade and the makeup of your teeth.
