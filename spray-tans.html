How it works

  1. Book a time online

    Pick a slot Monday to Thursday 9am–7pm or Friday and Saturday 9am–5pm. No card needed to book.

  2. Arrive clean and bare

    Shower, shave, and exfoliate a few hours before. No lotion, makeup, or deodorant. Bring dark, loose clothes.

  3. Pay at the desk, then glow

    Your spray takes about fifteen minutes. Pay when you come in. Rinse at the time for your solution.

Before and after your spray

Before

  • Book for two days before your event so the color settles.
  • Shower, shave, and exfoliate several hours before your appointment.
  • Come with a clean body and face. No makeup, lotion, or deodorant.
  • Wear or bring dark, loose clothing to change into afterward so the tan doesn't rub off.

After

  • Regular solution: wait 10–12 hours before your first shower. Rapid solution: wait 4–6 hours.
  • Until that first shower, avoid all moisture, including washing hands, working out, saunas, and pools.
  • Then moisturize daily. Skip anything with petrolatum, which strips the tan. We recommend Hempz.
  • Don't exfoliate until you're ready to take the tan off.
