Before you go

Most members who cancel just need a break. You have two options that keep your membership and your retail discount, and a third if you're sure.

Any change or cancellation must be in by 11:59pm on the 20th of the month to take effect before the next draft on the 1st.

Freeze it

Pause your membership for up to four months a year. No billing while frozen, and your level and pricing are waiting when you come back.

Call 385-326-8969 or ask at the desk.

Move to a lower level

Drop to Level 2 at $45 a month and keep tanning, keep your 15% retail discount, and skip the three-month restart later.

Call 385-326-8969 or ask at the desk.

Cancel

Fill out the form below. You'll get an email confirmation to keep. Billing and services end on the last day of the following month.
