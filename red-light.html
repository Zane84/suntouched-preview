Red light therapy

A full-body red light bed that boosts circulation, stimulates collagen and elastin, and calms inflammation. It emits zero UV and will not tan you, so it's safe for every skin type. Fifteen minutes, before your tan or on its own.

What it helps with

  • Fine lines, wrinkles, and skin firmness
  • Acne and acne scars
  • Redness, rosacea, eczema, and psoriasis
  • Age spots and under-eye circles
  • Stretch marks and scars
  • Sun-damaged skin and pore size
  • Wound healing and broken capillaries
  • Joint pain and inflammation

Results vary with age and skin condition. Red light is a cosmetic and wellness treatment, not a medical one.

How often

Most people see the best results over 8 to 12 weeks. Start with the full 15 minutes, three to five times a week for the first one to four weeks, then maintain.

Same day as tanning?

Yes. Use the red light bed before your tanning bed. It brings blood to the surface of the skin, which gives a better tan through increased oxidation of melanin.

Who should skip it

Anyone who is pregnant, has low blood pressure or epilepsy, is sensitive to light, or takes medication that increases light sensitivity.
